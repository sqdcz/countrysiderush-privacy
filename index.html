隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 18 日 开发者：衰气的船只

摘要：《乡野飞驰》不收集、不存储、不上传您的任何个人信息。本游戏无账号系统、无广告、无统计分析、无第三方 SDK；仅有的本地设置与游戏记录保存于您自己的设备。使用局域网联机时，IP 地址仅用于本次会话建立连接，不会被记录或外传。

一、引言

欢迎使用《乡野飞驰》（以下简称"本游戏"）。我们深知个人信息安全的重要性，并将尽全力保护其安全可靠。本隐私政策（以下简称"本政策"）旨在向您说明，在您下载、安装、使用本游戏的过程中，我们如何处理与您有关的信息。

特别提示：本游戏为一款以离线单机体验为主、支持局域网联机的赛车游戏。我们不收集您的任何个人信息。

二、我们收集的信息

我们不会收集您的任何个人信息。具体而言，本游戏：

  1. 不设账号系统。无需注册或登录，不会收集您的姓名、电子邮箱、手机号码、身份证件信息或任何其他身份识别信息；
  2. 不含广告。游戏内无任何广告，亦不集成广告 SDK，不会收集广告标识符（IDFA / GAID / OAID 等）；
  3. 无统计分析与崩溃诊断。不集成任何统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK，不会记录您的设备型号、操作系统版本、游戏时长、游玩行为等任何数据；
  4. 无网络请求。游戏在您的设备本地运行，除下述"局域网联机"功能外，不会向任何服务器发送数据。游戏所需的真实卫星影像、道路网络及高程数据均封装于安装包内，随游戏分发。

三、本地存储的数据

以下数据仅保存于您的设备本地，不会传输、同步或上传至任何服务器：

  • 游戏设置，包括画质等级、帧率上限、音量、按键绑定等；
  • 游戏记录，包括成就解锁状态与最佳圈速。

您可以通过卸载游戏或清除应用数据，随时删除上述信息。

四、局域网联机

当您主动使用"创建房间"或"加入房间"功能时，游戏将通过本地局域网（LAN）与其他玩家建立直接连接：

  1. 建立连接所需的 IP 地址仅用于本次联机会话，同一房间内的玩家彼此可见；
  2. 上述信息不会被记录、存储或转发至任何外部服务器，会话结束后即失效；
  3. 您可以随时退出房间或关闭游戏以终止连接。

五、数据来源

本游戏的世界基于公开数据构建，包括公开卫星影像、OpenStreetMap 公开道路网络数据及公开高程数据。上述数据均来源于公开渠道，不包含任何个人隐私信息。

六、儿童隐私

本游戏适合全年龄玩家。由于我们不收集任何个人信息，因此我们不会收集或处理儿童的个人信息。

七、第三方服务

本游戏未集成任何第三方服务、SDK、外部链接或应用内浏览器。

八、隐私政策的更新

我们可能会适时修订本政策。若变更将对您的权益产生重大影响，我们将通过游戏内更新说明等方式通知您。修改后的政策自公布之日起生效。

九、联系我们

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 18, 2026 Developer: 衰气的船只

Summary: Countryside Rush does not collect, store, or upload any of your personal information. The game has no account system, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. Settings and game records are stored only on your own device. During LAN multiplayer, IP addresses are used solely to establish the connection for that session and are never recorded or shared externally.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Countryside Rush (the "Game"). We take the security of personal information seriously and make every effort to protect it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information relating to you when you download, install, and use the Game.

Please note: the Game is primarily an offline single-player racing game with optional LAN multiplayer support. We do not collect any personal information from you.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, the Game:

  1. Has no account system. No registration or login is required, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, identification documents, or any other identifying information;
  2. Contains no advertising. There are no ads and no advertising SDKs, and we do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID / OAID, etc.);
  3. Has no analytics or crash diagnostics. No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are integrated, and we do not record your device model, operating system version, playtime, or gameplay behavior;
  4. Makes no network requests. The Game runs locally on your device and sends no data to any server, except for the LAN multiplayer function described below. The real-world satellite imagery, road network, and elevation data used by the Game are bundled within the installation package.

3. Data Stored Locally

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted, synced, or uploaded to any server:

  • Game settings, including graphics quality, FPS cap, volume, and key bindings;
  • Game records, including achievement status and best lap times.

You may delete this information at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing the app data.

4. LAN Multiplayer

When you voluntarily use the "Create Room" or "Join Room" feature, the Game establishes a direct connection with other players over your local area network (LAN):

  1. The IP address required to establish the connection is used only for the current multiplayer session and is visible to other players in the same room;
  2. This information is never recorded, stored, or forwarded to any external server, and becomes invalid once the session ends;
  3. You may terminate the connection at any time by leaving the room or closing the Game.

5. Data Sources

The Game world is built from public data, including public satellite imagery, public road network data from OpenStreetMap, and public elevation data. All of these sources are publicly available and contain no personal information.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game is suitable for players of all ages. Because we collect no personal information, we do not collect or process personal information from children.

7. Third-Party Services

The Game does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, external links, or in-app browsers.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this policy from time to time. If a change materially affects your rights, we will notify you through in-game update notes. The revised policy takes effect on the date it is published.
